Read my full response on Zionism and the CJA

The Chicago Jewish Alliance (CJA) — a self-proclaimed Zionist organization — has decided to publicly smear me for attending an anti-genocide rally. Let me be perfectly clear about what that means.

Zionism is not Judaism. It is a far-right nationalist ideology that demands an ethno-state for Jewish people carved out of Palestinian land. The movement emerged in the late 19th century with a single goal: establish a Jewish homeland in Palestine. In 1917, Britain issued the Balfour Declaration to Lord Rothschild, laying the groundwork for what was to come. In 1922, the League of Nations formalized British control through the Mandate for Palestine.

And then came the atrocities.

In 1933, Nazi Germany signed the Ha'avara Agreement with the Zionist Federation of Germany to facilitate Jewish emigration to Palestine. The Lehi gang — also known as the Stern Gang — was a Zionist paramilitary organization that operated in Mandatory Palestine. From November 1947 to May 1948, Zionist military forces forcibly expelled an estimated 750,000 to 1 million Palestinians from their homes. They call it The Nakba — the catastrophe. In September 1948, the Lehi gang assassinated Count Folke Bernadotte, a Swedish diplomat and UN mediator, who was simply trying to negotiate peace. And let's not forget: the Lehi gang tried to establish contact and reach an agreement with Nazi Germany. Many of its members were later absorbed into the newly formed Israel Defense Forces (IDF).

To call yourself a Zionist — to advocate for Zionism — is to advocate for the continued oppression of Palestinians. An oppression that started over 100 years ago and continues to this day.

And here's what the CJA and its defenders refuse to acknowledge: being anti-Zionist, being anti-Israel, does NOT make someone antisemitic. To claim otherwise is actual antisemitism — because it conflates Judaism itself with the brutal, oppressive state of Israel.

The CJA can post whatever they want. They can try to defame me. They can wrap themselves in the flag of Zionism and pretend they stand for justice. But the history is written. The blood is on the ground. And I will not be silenced by an organization that defends ethnic cleansing and calls it a homeland.

The bigger picture

One organization buys influence and the other manufactures consequences for anyone who questions it. Together they define the narrow range of positions a candidate in this district is permitted to hold. I am not taking their money, and I am not accepting those limits. Voters in IL-10 deserve a representative accountable to them — not to a lobby.
